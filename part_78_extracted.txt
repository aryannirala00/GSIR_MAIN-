PART 78 — GSIR SCIENTIFIC WORKFLOW ORCHESTRATION AND EXECUTION ARCHITECTURE

78.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR Workflow Orchestration Layer shall provide the execution infrastructure for scientific workflows, AI workflows, data-processing pipelines, experiments, simulations, evaluations and autonomous research tasks.

The architecture shall capture the complete computational process, including data, code, parameters, environment, workflow and execution state, because these elements are fundamental to reproducible computational research.
