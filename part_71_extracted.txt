PART 71 — SCIENTIFIC WORKFLOW ORCHESTRATION AND EXECUTION ENGINE

71.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR Scientific Workflow Orchestration Engine shall coordinate complex research workflows involving data acquisition, document processing, scientific computation, AI reasoning, experiments, validation, knowledge updates, and reporting.

The workflow engine shall make research processes:

Deterministic where possible

Reproducible

Versioned

Observable

Recoverable

Auditable

Parallelizable

Scalable

Scientific workflow systems are valuable because they can automatically capture computational provenance, connect results to the processes that produced them, and support rerunning computational tasks.

Reusable workflows should expose machine-readable metadata describing their inputs, outputs, dependencies, versions, and provenance. FAIR principles for research software explicitly emphasize findability, interoperability, reusability, and detailed provenance.
