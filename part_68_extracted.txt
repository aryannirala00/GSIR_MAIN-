PART 68 — SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE GRAPH AND RESEARCH KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION ARCHITECTURE

68.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR Scientific Knowledge Graph shall provide the structured knowledge layer connecting scientific entities, concepts, evidence, experiments, observations, models, datasets, publications, researchers, hypotheses and validated results.

The knowledge graph shall transform disconnected research information into an interconnected scientific representation.

Research

   │

   ├── Concepts

   ├── Hypotheses

   ├── Experiments

   ├── Observations

   ├── Datasets

   ├── Models

   ├── Publications

   ├── Methods

   ├── Evidence

   └── Results

A provenance-aware knowledge representation is important because scientific results must remain traceable to the processes and entities that produced them. 

END OF 68.1
