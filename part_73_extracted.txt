PART 73 — SCIENTIFIC METADATA, SEMANTIC INTEROPERABILITY, AND RESEARCH OBJECT CATALOG ENGINE

73.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR Metadata and Semantic Interoperability Engine shall provide the machine-readable descriptive layer connecting datasets, documents, experiments, models, workflows, software, evidence, claims, publications, agents, and knowledge.

The engine shall ensure that GSIR research objects are:

Findable

Accessible

Interoperable

Reusable

Machine-Readable

Semantically Connected

Versioned

Provenance-Aware

The FAIR principles explicitly emphasize persistent identifiers, rich metadata, interoperable vocabularies, qualified relationships, licensing, provenance, and domain standards.

73.96 FINAL ARCHITECTURAL REQUIREMENT

The GSIR Metadata and Semantic Interoperability Engine shall enforce:

EVERY RESEARCH OBJECT MUST HAVE AN IDENTITY.

EVERY IMPORTANT OBJECT MUST HAVE VERSIONED METADATA.

EVERY IMPORTANT RELATIONSHIP MUST BE EXPLICIT.

EVERY SEMANTIC MAPPING MUST HAVE A DEFINED CONFIDENCE.

EVERY EXTERNAL TERM MUST RETAIN ITS ORIGINAL FORM.

EVERY ONTOLOGY DEPENDENCY MUST BE VERSIONED.

EVERY IMPORTANT OBJECT MUST BE DISCOVERABLE BY MACHINES.

EVERY EXPORTED OBJECT MUST RETAIN ENOUGH METADATA FOR INTERPRETATION.

EVERY METADATA CHANGE MUST BE TRACEABLE.

EVERY SCIENTIFIC OBJECT MUST REMAIN LINKED TO ITS PROVENANCE.

The resulting architecture shall make GSIR's scientific data, software, workflows, experiments, models, evidence, claims, and knowledge machine-discoverable, semantically interoperable, version-aware, provenance-linked, and reusable across research environments. FAIR guidance applies these principles not only to datasets but also to workflows and other research objects, making metadata and interoperability foundational to the complete GSIR architecture.
