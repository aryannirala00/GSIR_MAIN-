PART 76 — SCIENTIFIC DATA GOVERNANCE, SECURITY, ACCESS CONTROL, PRIVACY AND COMPLIANCE ARCHITECTURE

76.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR Data Governance Architecture shall define how scientific data, research objects, knowledge, files, experiments, workflows, AI inputs, AI outputs, metadata, and provenance are controlled throughout their complete lifecycle.

The governance system shall ensure:

Security

Privacy

Ownership

Access Control

Data Quality

Provenance

Retention

Compliance

Interoperability

Scientific Integrity

FAIR principles emphasize that research objects should be findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable, while sensitive data may require authentication and authorization and still retain rich metadata. ([nature.com](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618))

76.98 FINAL GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE

The GSIR governance system shall ensure that access to scientific information is determined by policy rather than convenience.

The complete decision path shall be:

WHO

↓

WANTS TO DO WHAT

↓

TO WHICH RESOURCE

↓

FOR WHAT PURPOSE

↓

UNDER WHICH CLASSIFICATION

↓

UNDER WHICH POLICY

↓

WITH WHICH PERMISSION

↓

IN WHICH ENVIRONMENT

↓

WITH WHICH DATA

↓

UNDER WHICH AUDIT REQUIREMENTS

Only when all applicable controls permit the operation shall execution proceed.

END OF 76.98

76.99 FINAL DATA GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENT

GSIR shall preserve the following fundamental rules:

NO UNAUTHORIZED DATA ACCESS.

NO UNCONTROLLED DATA SHARING.

NO UNTRACKED SENSITIVE DATA PROCESSING.

NO UNGOVERNED AI ACCESS.

NO SILENT POLICY OVERRIDES.

NO UNEXPLAINED DATA DELETION.

NO LOSS OF PROVENANCE.

NO CROSS-TENANT DATA LEAKAGE.

NO TRUST BASED ONLY ON NETWORK LOCATION.

NO AI AGENT WITH UNBOUNDED AUTHORITY.

The governance architecture shall operate across the full research lifecycle:

COLLECT

↓

CLASSIFY

↓

STORE

↓

PROCESS

↓

ANALYZE

↓

SHARE

↓

PUBLISH

↓

ARCHIVE

↓

DELETE / RETAIN

At every stage, the system shall preserve appropriate security, privacy, ownership, provenance, licensing, access and scientific-integrity controls.
