PART 75 — SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE GRAPH AND EVIDENCE GRAPH ENGINE

75.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR Scientific Knowledge Graph Engine shall provide the structured knowledge layer connecting scientific entities, observations, measurements, experiments, datasets, publications, evidence, claims, models, workflows, researchers, and derived conclusions.

The engine shall transform validated information into an interconnected graph while preserving source context, uncertainty, provenance, temporal state, and evidence.

Modern scientific data interoperability increasingly relies on semantic representations, ontologies, qualified relationships, and provenance to make research objects interpretable and reusable across systems.

75.96 FINAL ARCHITECTURAL REQUIREMENT

The GSIR Scientific Knowledge Graph Engine shall enforce:

EVERY IMPORTANT KNOWLEDGE OBJECT MUST HAVE AN IDENTITY.

EVERY IMPORTANT CLAIM MUST HAVE TRACEABLE EVIDENCE.

EVERY IMPORTANT RELATIONSHIP MUST HAVE PROVENANCE.

SOURCE STATEMENTS MUST REMAIN DISTINGUISHABLE FROM GSIR CONCLUSIONS.

CONTRADICTORY KNOWLEDGE MUST BE PRESERVED, NOT SILENTLY DELETED.

UNKNOWN MUST REMAIN DIFFERENT FROM FALSE.

CORRELATION MUST REMAIN DIFFERENT FROM CAUSATION.

INFERENCE MUST REMAIN DIFFERENT FROM DIRECT OBSERVATION.

EVERY IMPORTANT KNOWLEDGE STATE MUST BE VERSIONED.

EVERY RETRACTION MUST PROPAGATE THROUGH IMPACT ANALYSIS.

EVERY AI-GENERATED CONCLUSION MUST BE TRACEABLE TO ITS EVIDENCE.

EVERY SCIENTIFIC ANSWER MUST BE CAPABLE OF TRACING BACK TO ITS SOURCES.

The resulting GSIR knowledge layer shall provide the foundation for evidence-grounded scientific reasoning, contradiction analysis, multi-hop discovery, provenance-aware AI, research memory, hypothesis generation, and eventually autonomous scientific investigation. Its design shall preserve the distinction between source information, extracted evidence, validated claims, and inferred knowledge rather than collapsing them into a single undifferentiated graph.
