PART 69 — SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE GRAPH STORAGE, QUERY, AND PROVENANCE IMPLEMENTATION

69.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR knowledge graph storage layer shall provide persistent, versioned, provenance-aware storage for scientific entities, claims, relationships, evidence, sources, hypotheses, experiments and derived knowledge.

The implementation shall prioritize:

- Traceability

- Version control

- Evidence linkage

- Graph traversal

- Scientific queryability

- Efficient retrieval

- Reproducibility

- Controlled updates

Scientific knowledge graphs benefit from combining structured relationships with evidence and provenance, particularly for reproducibility and verification.

GSIR shall separate:

Scientific Knowledge

        +

        +

Provenance

        +

The search index and vector index shall never become the authoritative source of scientific truth.

The authoritative knowledge state shall remain in the primary knowledge storage layer.

69.76 FINAL ARCHITECTURAL REQUIREMENT

The GSIR knowledge layer shall be designed around one fundamental rule:

Every important scientific statement must remain connected to its identity, evidence, source, provenance, version and validation state.

The knowledge graph is therefore not merely a database of scientific facts.

It is the persistent, traceable representation of:

WHAT GSIR KNOWS

+

WHY GSIR KNOWS IT

+

WHERE IT CAME FROM

+

WHEN IT WAS VALID

+

WHAT SUPPORTS IT

+

WHAT CONTRADICTS IT

+

HOW IT WAS DERIVED

+

WHICH VERSION PRODUCED IT

This architecture enables GSIR to maintain scientific knowledge as a continuously evolving but historically traceable research asset.
