PART 70 — SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE GRAPH PROVENANCE, LINEAGE, AND REPRODUCIBILITY ENGINE

70.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR Provenance and Lineage Engine shall maintain the complete history of how scientific knowledge is created, transformed, validated, updated, and consumed.

Scientific knowledge graphs require explicit connections between assertions, evidence, sources, and provenance to support reproducibility and trustworthy knowledge discovery. 

The engine shall answer:

Where did this knowledge come from?

What data produced it?

Which process transformed it?

Which model generated it?

Which researcher or agent performed the operation?

Which software version was used?

Which evidence supports it?

Which version of the knowledge existed at that time?

Can the process be reproduced?

END OF 70.1

70.60 PROVENANCE STORAGE STRATEGY

or:

A hybrid approach may separate large provenance histories from the operational graph while maintaining stable references between them. Provenance management systems have demonstrated both graph-level and relational approaches for storing and retrieving provenance.
