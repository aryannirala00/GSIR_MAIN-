PART 74 — SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE GRAPH AND ONTOLOGY ENGINE

74.1 PURPOSE

The GSIR Scientific Knowledge Graph and Ontology Engine shall provide the semantic intelligence layer that connects scientific entities, concepts, observations, measurements, experiments, datasets, publications, models, workflows, evidence, and claims.

The engine shall convert validated information into a structured, queryable representation while preserving provenance, uncertainty, temporal context, source identity, and semantic relationships.

Semantic interoperability increasingly depends on explicit ontologies, vocabularies, identifiers, and machine-actionable metadata rather than simple exchange of files.

74.99 FINAL ARCHITECTURAL REQUIREMENT

The GSIR Knowledge Graph and Ontology Engine shall enforce:

EVERY IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC ENTITY MUST HAVE A STABLE IDENTITY.

EVERY IMPORTANT RELATIONSHIP MUST HAVE A DEFINED SEMANTIC MEANING.

EVERY CLAIM MUST BE DISTINGUISHED FROM ITS EVIDENCE.

EVERY INFERENCE MUST BE DISTINGUISHED FROM AN OBSERVATION.

EVERY IMPORTANT ASSERTION MUST BE TRACEABLE TO ITS SOURCE.
